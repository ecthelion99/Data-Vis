Australian Energy Generation

Energy generation throughout the Australian states have somewhat diverse sources. Over-time generation has become more diverse. Most states still have a significant reliance on either coal or natural gas. The two exceptions are Tasmania, which has almost no reliance on coal or gas, and South Australia, whose major generation source is wind and solar (a significant shift from 2019).

Australia's 2030 renewable goal is 82% renewable generation. Only Tasmania has achieved this (although Tasmania imports from the National Electricity Market) and South Australia is making good progress whilst the other states are lagging.
